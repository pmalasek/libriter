# Libriter Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

Libriter is a mobile client for the self-hosted Libriter audiobook server. This policy explains how the Libriter mobile app for iOS and Android handles your information.

## We don't collect your data

The developer of Libriter does not collect, store or have access to any of your personal data. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services, and it does not share data with third parties.

## Data sent to your Libriter server

The app talks only to the Libriter server whose address you enter when you sign in. That server is run by you or by whoever gave you access to it. The app sends the server:

- your login (email or username) and password, when you sign in
- your device name, so you can recognize the session on the server
- your listening progress and history (which books you play and where you stopped)

Whoever runs that server controls this data. To access or delete it, contact your server's administrator.

## Data stored on your device

- A sign-in token, kept in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android)
- Books you download, and in offline mode a copy of your library (books, authors, series and listening progress)
- Your app settings

Signing out removes your sign-in token. Downloaded books can be deleted in the app, and uninstalling the app removes all data stored on the device.

## Children

Libriter is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
